# MCLA bench flow 2, driving (tools/bench.py): free roam in traffic, then a long REAL drive. 360 s.
# Replaces bench_drive.txt (a parked bike against a wall, see below). The pattern of quick_drive2.txt continues to 444 s;
# `seconds` stays 360 (bench.py measures from `-> free-roam` + 20 s to the end). Only the first 118 s are verified: the
# long drive is unverified beyond that (a 360 s run is made with the final defaults, see docs/METRICS.md).
# The boot (first 49 s) is the one of bench_drive.txt, so `-> free-roam` and the windows compare with the old rows.
# Use: python tools/run_game.py --flow bench_drive2 --config Release [--cvar k=v]... [--screenshot-every 5]
# Format: <second>:<button>[+<button>...][/<hold ms>], '#' comments; seconds count from process start.
#
# WHY THE OLD FLOWS DID NOT MOVE (quick_drive.txt / bench_drive.txt, fixture after_intro, a motorbike): the save parks the
# bike on a pavement facing the street, with a low wall on the far side. The old flows hold rt for 14 s with a half-second
# steer at the end of the slice: the bike is at 38 mph two seconds in, hits the wall about 3 s ahead and then sits against it
# with the rear wheel smoking: 0 mph on the speedometer in every screenshot of every run (t60 / t80 / t100 of t1-m2-2000u,
# ov-*, r1-*). Nothing in the script ever reverses or steers away from an obstacle.
#
# WHAT THIS FLOW DOES (a pad script cannot read the game, so it is open loop and built to recover from what it hits):
#   boot  = the old timeline, unchanged: Start/300 at 28, 30, 32, 34, B taps at 40..49 (close a pause a late Start opened).
#           Kept on purpose: the title shows 20 s after the process start in one boot and 28 s in the next (legals and logos
#           of varying length), so a Start before 28 s is lost in some boots. `-> free-roam` comes at 35-37 s.
#   open  = T+49.5, 11.1 s at full throttle: a 1.0 s right turn out of the spawn onto Selma Ave, a 0.4 s left tap at 52.3 that
#           keeps the bike on the road side of the Food Mart corner (without it the bike clips that corner and wedges),
#           then Highland Ave north at 70-75 mph to the Hollywood Blvd crossing. The first 15 s are the same streets at the
#           same speeds in every run (9-18 mph at t50, 47-61 at t55, 70-74 at t60, 11-29 at t65).
#   units = 6.2 s each, from 60.6: a 2.0 s reverse pulse (lt=1 with the stick locked to alternating sides, right first), then
#           4.0 s of throttle at rt=0.7 with a 1.2 s counter-lock at its start. Moving, the bike brakes and reverses a little;
#           against a wall, a car or a kerb it backs off and the full-lock forward start slides it along the obstacle, which
#           is what frees it; the next unit turns the other way. The end of a pulse is in reverse gear (1-12 mph).
#   modals= b/300 0.6 s into every reverse pulse closes a BACK-type modal (the DELIVERY job dialog the bike opens by riding
#           over a job marker: BACK = B, ACCEPT = A; it freezes the drive until closed). Every second unit an a/200 follows
#           0.7 s later for the 'DAMAGED OUT / QUICK FIX' modal that only A answers (a long blind drive reaches it, ~282 s
#           in W2.1) and for the 'ARRESTED' summary: the B before it keeps A from landing on a DELIVERY dialog (A = accept).
#
# WHAT WAS TRIED AND REJECTED (runs dr-* in the work folder, 1080p and 2000p; speedometer read in the screenshots):
#   * full throttle in the units (65-90 mph): the bike wedges for good at the Food Mart corner or a building wall (reverse gear
#     shows 0 mph, rear wheel spinning, 40+ s), and speeding past a police car starts a pursuit and an arrest: 1 of 4 runs held.
#   * rt=0.45: never wedged and never arrested, but a 2-9 mph crawl (gear 1). rt=0.6-0.7 gives 25-45 mph ('CR' lights up at
#     the speed limit). rt=0.52-0.6 in 8 s units: police in every run by t80, 43-93 % of the samples above zero.
#   * 1.2 s and 1.8 s reverse pulses (stuck 12 s at a diner wall; a standstill at the end of every pulse), a 0.7 s counter-lock
#     (the bike stays pinned), first reverse pulse locked to the left (the forward start then turns right into the Hollywood
#     & Highland forecourt, a pedestrian dead end: police pull the bike over and arrest it, ~20 s lost; seen in 1 of 3 runs).
#   * no button frees a wedged bike: X is the nitrous (a flash and a camera kick); Y, LB, RB, L3, R3 and A do nothing.
#
# MEASURED (2000p, shadow 640, vrs 0, saturated cvars of docs/native NOTES: r23-base runs; speedometer read in the t50, t55
# ... t115 screenshots, 14 samples a run): above 0 mph in 93 %, 86 %, 86 % of the samples (above 10 mph: 64 %, 64 %, 71 %).
